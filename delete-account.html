Delete Your Club Match Account

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your account and profile information
  • All match results and weigh-in data you created
  • Your club memberships and associations
  • League standings and fixture data you entered
  • Any cached data on your device
Note: Data shared with clubs you belonged to may remain visible to other club members as part of historical match records, but your personal account information will be removed.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Club Match account, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to covchump@covchump.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include in your email:
    • The email address associated with your Club Match account
    • Your name (as shown in the app)
    • Any clubs you belong to (optional, helps us locate your account faster)
  4. We will respond within 7 days to confirm your request
  5. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days of your request
