Privacy Policy for Club Match

Last updated: February 19, 2025

Introduction

Club Match ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Club Match mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address and password when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Your name and club membership details
  • Match Data: Fishing match results, weights, dates, venues, and peg numbers
  • Club Data: Club names and member information you enter

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • App Usage: Features you use, match data you enter
  • Firebase Authentication Data: User authentication tokens

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App functionality
  • Manage your account and authentication
  • Store and display your fishing match results
  • Enable offline functionality
  • Sync data across your devices
  • Improve and optimize the App

Data Storage

Server Storage

  • Match data, club information, and league results are stored on our privately hosted secure backend server
  • Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Server access is restricted to authorized personnel only

Server Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and our server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Access Control: Server access is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular Updates: Our server software is regularly updated with security patches
  • Monitoring: Server activity is monitored for suspicious behavior
  • Backups: Regular automated backups ensure data can be recovered in case of hardware failure
  • Limited Data Retention: We only retain data necessary for app functionality

Authentication

  • User authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google)
  • We do not store your password; Firebase manages all authentication securely
  • Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Local Storage

  • Your device caches data locally using Room Database for offline functionality
  • Cached data includes match results, club members, and league information
  • Local data is synchronized with our servers when you're online
  • Cached data is stored securely on your device and deleted when you uninstall the app

Offline Functionality

  • The app maintains a local database for offline access
  • Changes made offline are queued and synced when connectivity is restored
  • Pending syncs are stored locally until successfully uploaded to the server

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share data with:

  • Firebase/Google: For authentication services
  • Club Members: Match results are visible to other members of your club
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at: covchump@covchump.com

Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • You can delete your account at any time
  • Deleted data is removed from our servers within 30 days

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) - For secure user login and account management
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services - Required for app distribution and updates
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Retrofit & OkHttp - For secure API communications (client libraries only, no data collection)

We do NOT use:

  • Analytics or tracking services
  • Advertising networks
  • Third-party data brokers

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy in the App.
